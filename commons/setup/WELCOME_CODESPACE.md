# Welcome to your Commons Engineering cloud workspace

You're working **entirely in your browser** — nothing is installed on your
computer. This is the path for anyone who can't (or would rather not) install
software locally.

Everything you need is already here: the tools, your Commons OS workspace, and
your AI working agent.

## One step to begin

1. Open the **Claude** panel — the spark/Claude icon in the left sidebar
   (or top-right of the editor).
2. **Sign in** when asked — it's a button, no codes to copy. No account yet?
   Choose **Sign up**.
3. Type: **`set up my Commons environment from ONBOARDING.md`**

That's it. Your agent takes over from there — it sets your identity, builds your
venture workspace, and connects you to the shared knowledge of Commons
Engineering. You can ask it anything, any time. There's nothing else you need
to read first.
