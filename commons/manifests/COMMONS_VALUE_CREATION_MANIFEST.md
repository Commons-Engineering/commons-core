*The universal architecture of collaborative value creation*

**Status:** Draft — foundation for all domain essentials packs
**Level:** Pattern 1 — The Commons (Orbit 1)
**Organisation:** cloudsters FEDERATION

## §1 The thesis

There is **one** architecture of collaborative value creation. Business, Health, Urban, Life, Ecology — and any commons yet to come — are **expressions of the same system**, not separate family-islands. A Commons Engineer or Incubator learns *one* architecture and applies it in any domain. From the perspective of the complex value-creation system, the similarity across domains is very high — because it is the *same* system.

This manifest defines that shared layer: the **domain-agnostic Value Stream Families** every commons runs.

## §2 The specialization gradient — D0 · VSD → VSF → VS

The model has a **centre and four arms** (a Purpose Spiral): **D0**, the Singularity at the centre, and the four dimensions **D1–D4** spiralling out from it.

| Level | What it is | Universality |
|---|---|---|
| **D0** — the Singularity | the point + the reflexive **consciousness of the whole system** (Orbit 0); one seed family | **the centre** |
| **VSD** — Value Stream Dimension | the four dimensions D1–D4 (the four Ps) spiralling out of D0 | **fully universal** |
| **VSF** — Value Stream Family | the universal transformations of any commons | **universal — THIS canon** |
| **VS** — Value Stream | the concrete stream | **domain / industry specialization begins here** |

Specialization increases outward from D0. The **families are universal**; the **domain's Stallgeruch lives at the VS level** (and the industry *process* concentrates in D4). This resolves the D4-industry-frame: same D0–D4 families everywhere → instances are comparable as a System of Systems; only the value streams beneath differ.

**D0 is not a fifth agent.** It is the Singularity the **Purpose Agent (D1)** guards — its Monitor / Correct / Supervise reflex, the system's awareness of itself as a whole. Identity lives at the centre (D0), not as a peer function.

### §2.1 The scope rule *(the discipline that keeps families universal)*

> **A Value Stream Family must be broad enough to hold ALL its value streams across every stakeholder, value, journey, and asset in the commons.** If a name captures only one value (a clinical *case*), one material (only what must be *sterile*), one path (only *admit→discharge*), it is a Value *Stream* wearing a family's clothes — too narrow. The family names the whole; the specialization lives one level down.

## §3 The 13 universal families

| Level | Family | Universal transformation |
|---|---|---|
| **D0 · Perception & Mandate** *(the Singularity)* | **Perception to Mandate** | the system perceives itself as a whole and holds its irreducible mandate — identity, boundary, legitimacy, ethics, the reason to exist |
| **D1 · Purpose & System** | **Purpose to Portfolio** | translate purpose into a coherent portfolio of commitments |
| | **Value to Sustainability** | capture, account, reinvest value so the system endures |
| | **Portfolio to System** | architect the whole as a coherent system-of-systems *("System" = the whole operating system incl. the value model, not just IT)* |
| **D2 Participation & Relationship** | **Participant to Community** | cultivate individual participants into a thriving community |
| | **Collaboration to Automation** | evolve collaboration (human + agent) into reliable, increasingly automated patterns |
| | **Contributor to Succession** | steward the lifecycle of the intelligent contributors (human **or** AI agent) who shape the commons — find → onboard → keep relevant → succeed the role |
| **D3 Proposition & Exchange** | **Discovery to Usage** | conceive, craft, deliver, evolve the offerings |
| | **Interest to Relationship** | cultivate awareness into a lasting 1:1 relationship |
| | **Proposition to Audience** | the value proposition reaches its receiving & interacting community (1:n) |
| **D4 Production & Resilience** | **Demand to Fulfillment** | orchestrate production & delivery from demand to fulfilled promise |
| | **Source to Alliance** | supplier & input relationships: sourcing → partnership *(consumables & inputs live here)* |
| | **Acquire to Retire** | steward the full lifecycle of the durable infrastructure / means of production |

*(business-essentials' 14 cross-industry families were effectively the first draft of this canon; only "Value to Profit → Value to Sustainability" needed neutralizing, and its two lifecycle families merged — see §3.2.)*

### §3.2 The agency line — D2 (contributors) vs D4 (tools)

D2's two lifecycle families (business's *Hire to Retire* + *Welcome to Transition*) **merged into one — `Contributor to Succession`** *(chosen by a rigorous blind naming contest: 110 candidates, 8 anti-anchored generators, 4 weighted judges)*. The reason is a principle:

> The subject of this family is the **intelligent CONTRIBUTOR — human OR machine/AI agent — with agency**. That agency is the line between **D2 (contributors who participate and shape)** and **D4 `Acquire to Retire` (tools & infrastructure without agency)**. The boundary between human, machine, and embedded intelligence is **dynamic** — a tool gaining intelligence migrates across it.

For an intelligent contributor, the old split (whole-membership arc vs task/role cycle) is *one* lifecycle — those become value streams beneath. Enterprise-legible names like "Hire"/"Talent" were rejected because they re-narrow the subject to humans.

### §3.1 Families vs qualities — why the canon is 13, not 16

We tested whether D3 and D4 need a fourth family to deliver the "**& Exchange**" and "**& Resilience**" halves of their dimension names. Both were rejected — and the reason is a principle:

- **Exchange is not a family — it is the ENDPOINT of the relationship families.** The proposition, once it enters a relationship (*Interest to Relationship* — the 1:1 agreement) and reaches its audience (*Proposition to Audience* — the 1:n market exchange), *is* exchanged; the revenue is captured in *Value to Sustainability*. A separate "Exchange" family would only split the natural endpoint of Interest to Relationship.
- **Resilience is not a family — it is a QUALITY that pervades all of D4** (and the whole system): the entire infrastructure, production, and sourcing must be *resilient*. It is not a function performed alongside the others; it is a property they all embody, most intensely in D4.

This clarifies a distinction the canon relies on: **families are FUNCTIONS the commons performs; qualities are PERVASIVE PROPERTIES it embodies.** Exactly as **Justice** is not a fifth dimension but a quality pervading all four, **Resilience** and **Vitality** are qualities — not families. These 13 are the functions. **The canon is 13 (4/3/3/3), by design, not omission** *(D2 = 3 after the lifecycle merge, §3.2)*.

## §4 The proof — one family, five domains

The architecture holds because each family expresses across every domain. Example — **Value to Sustainability**:

| Domain | Expression |
|---|---|
| Business | revenue → profit → reinvestment |
| Health | DRG case **+** research funding **+** teaching allocation **+** donations → reinvestment |
| Urban | taxes + grants → commons fund |
| Life | income + energy → wealth & vitality |
| Ecology | ecosystem services → regenerative capacity |

The full 5-domain matrix for all 13 families lives in the canon JSON — the Gesamtübersicht at Pattern 1.

## §5 The naming principle — enterprise-legible over esoteric

The near-future revenue is **organisations and the people who work with organisations** (enterprise architects, advisors, boards). They must not get lost in esoteric terminology.

| Rule | |
|---|---|
| **Enterprise-legible over esoteric** | family names are recognisable value-stream vocabulary, never Commons-poetry |
| **Gold standard = the industry-recognised name** | Source-to-Pay/Alliance, Hire-to-Retire, Demand-to-Fulfillment, Acquire-to-Retire are strong *because* they are recognised; abstract inventions are the weak ones |
| **"A to B" is a FAMILY-altitude grammar** | the family is a transformation arc; **value streams are descriptive noun phrases**, never forced "A to B" |
| **Do not drift** | toward Legacy / Vitality / Reciprocity-style poetic endpoints |

## §6 How domain packs inherit the canon

Each essentials pack (business, health, urban, life, ecology) **inherits the 13 families unchanged** and specializes at the VS level (plus the D4 industry process). A pack does not invent its own family layer; it expresses this one. Comparability across every instance — a hospital, a company, a city, a life, an ecosystem — is preserved because they share the same D1–D4 families.
